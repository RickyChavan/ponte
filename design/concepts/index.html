Three new directions for the homepage

Each concept is a working page using the same brand and daisyUI theme as the built site. They are sketches to react to, not finished designs. Pending facts are marked placeholder; nothing here invents clients, numbers or people.

A

Pod builder

Visitors compose their own 4 to 6 person pod from role counters, see it summarised, then talk to us about that pod.

Best for: founders who want to feel in control; makes the abstract "pod" concrete.

Watch out: may imply pricing or availability we have not decided; skip if the team is not ready to talk about specific roles.

Open concept A
B

Trust first

An editorial page for a company with no case studies: privacy diagram, terms in writing, people, FAQ that answers the hard questions.

Best for: sceptical buyers (CTOs, finance heads, incubators) who need legitimacy.

Watch out: needs real decisions and real people from the founder before it can go live; many placeholders today.

Open concept B
C

Quick contact

Shortest path to a human: WhatsApp, a booking call or a three-field callback form, with an English/Hindi switch.

Best for: MSME owners on phones who prefer talking to typing.

Watch out: needs a real WhatsApp/phone number and someone to answer; Hindi copy needs a native reviewer.
