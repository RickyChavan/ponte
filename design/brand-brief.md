# Ponte: Brand Brief (draft v0.1)

## What Ponte is
Management-as-a-service for MSMEs and startups. Ponte provides the right team to run and grow parts of a business (operations, finance, HR, sales, etc.) so founders do not have to hire and manage everything themselves.

## Positioning
**Ponte is the bridge between your ambition and the right team.** (*Ponte* means "bridge" in Italian and Portuguese.)

## Audience
1. **Primary: founders and owners** of MSMEs and early-stage startups. They are stretched thin, wearing every hat, and know growth needs people they cannot yet hire full-time.
2. **Secondary: talent and partners.** Professionals and firms who want to be part of Ponte's bench. They get a lighter path (a short strip and a form), not equal weight.

## Voice and tone (warm and approachable)
- Plain, human language. No consultant jargon.
- Speak to the founder as "you"; Ponte as "we".
- Confident but not boastful: show how it works, don't overpromise.
- Short sentences. One idea per paragraph.
- Avoid: "synergy", "leverage", "end-to-end solutions", guarantees of results.

## Tagline options (pick one)
1. Your growth team, on call.
2. The right team, right when you need it.
3. Build the bridge to your next stage.

## Visual direction
Warm cream background, deep teal text, terracotta accent. Rounded shapes, generous whitespace, real photography of people working (when available). Bridge motif: a simple arch used sparingly in the logo and section dividers. See [design-tokens.md](design-tokens.md).

## Logo direction (not generated yet)
Wordmark "ponte" in lowercase rounded type, with the "o" or an underline drawn as a small arch. Generate concepts only after you confirm the brand direction.

## Rules for content
- No invented client names, numbers or testimonials in anything that could go live. Wireframes use clearly labelled placeholders.
- Pricing is not shown publicly; the CTA is "Talk to us about pricing".

## Open questions
- Domain: ponte.co.in (bought). Contact email can use it, e.g. hello@ponte.co.in (confirm before launch).
- Exact service list Ponte offers at launch.
- Founders' real case studies, once available.
