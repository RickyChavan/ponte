# Visitor personas for the Ponte website simulation

**All six people below are fictional and illustrative.** They are archetypes built from the founder's brief and general knowledge of Indian startup and MSME buyers. They are not real people, and nothing they "say" is evidence about real customers. Findings from the simulation are hypotheses to test with real visitors and analytics.

Each visitor browses the Ponte website (see `site-snapshot.md`) and decides whether to contact Ponte, leave, or come back later.

## 1. Rohan Mehta, 29, first-time founder (pre-seed SaaS, Bengaluru)
- Two co-founders, one product engineer, burning savings. Needs 2 engineers, someone for outbound sales and a part-time finance person but cannot afford full-time hires or recruitment fees.
- Finds Ponte through a LinkedIn post shared by an incubator friend, on his phone during a commute.
- Cares about: speed to start, monthly cost certainty, whether the people are good, not being locked in. Skims; reads headings and buttons only. Impatient with forms.
- Fears: paying for "pods" that are junior people with a manager on top; hidden costs.

## 2. Sunita Joshi, 52, owner of a family auto-parts MSME (Pune, 60 staff)
- Runs the business on WhatsApp, Tally and phone calls. Her son nags her to "digitise" sales and accounts. Not a tech person; reads English but prefers Hindi/Marathi.
- Arrives from a Google search recommended by her CA, on a mid-range Android phone, sometimes on slow 4G.
- Cares about: trust, a real person to talk to, plain words, a phone number, knowing the people are in India, GST/Udyam-style legitimacy.
- Fears: being sold something she cannot judge; fancy words ("pod", "management as a service"); giving her number to a stranger.

## 3. Karthik Raman, 38, CTO of a 25-person B2B startup (Chennai)
- Currently uses freelancers and one agency; quality is inconsistent. Considering a managed pod for the next product build.
- Arrives from a Google search for "dedicated development team India" and has three other tabs open (an agency, a marketplace, an Indian offshore vendor).
- Cares about: who the engineers are, how code ownership, IP and security are handled, process, references, price range, exit terms. Wants specifics and proof.
- Fears: vendor lock-in, IP leakage, a sales call that goes nowhere. Will leave quickly if the site has no proof or specifics.

## 4. Neha Kulkarni, 44, COO/CFO at a 120-person logistics company (Mumbai)
- Her board wants operations and finance cleaned up. She is sceptical of outsourcing parts of finance/HR because of confidential data.
- Arrives from an email forwarded by a peer. Reads on a laptop, carefully, looking for the privacy and legal story.
- Cares about: data isolation, NDAs, DPDP Act compliance, who the lawyer is, where data lives, audit rights, what happens if a pod member leaves.
- Fears: a vague "our lawyer reviews contracts" claim, a missing privacy policy, a dead "Privacy policy" link.

## 5. Arjun Nair, 33, senior DevOps engineer (Hyderabad), curious about joining
- Tired of a service company; wants autonomy and fair working hours, which attracts him to "European work culture".
- Arrives from a recruiter message mentioning Ponte, on a laptop at night.
- Cares about: pay clarity, who he would work for, stability of a new firm, remote/hybrid, growth, whether the culture claims are real.
- Fears: a body-shop dressed up with nice copy; no information about the people behind Ponte; a talent form that disappears into nothing.

## 6. Meera Iyer, 47, programme manager at a startup incubator (Delhi)
- Looks for credible partners to recommend to her cohort of 40 startups. Her reputation is at stake in every recommendation.
- Arrives via a LinkedIn message from a Ponte team member; looks at the site on a laptop before replying.
- Cares about: legitimacy (registered company, named leadership, lawyer, track record), a partner programme or referral route, clear pricing logic, someone she can call.
- Fears: recommending an unproven company. Will not refer anyone without seeing real people and proof.
