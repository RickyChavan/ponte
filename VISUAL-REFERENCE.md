## Photography Style (Not Style Examples)

**What it should feel like:**
- ✅ Contemporary corporate photography (like LinkedIn/TechCrunch hero shots)
- ✅ Authentic and real (genuine people, genuine smiles)
- ✅ Professional but approachable (not stiff, not overly casual)
- ✅ Warm and inviting (natural lighting, human connection)
- ✅ Modern Indian context (visible architecture, fashion mix)

**What to avoid:**
- ❌ Overly posed or stiff corporate shots
- ❌ Generic international stock photography
- ❌ Artificial, over-filtered, unrealistic appearance
- ❌ Cold or clinical lighting
- ❌ Heavy-handed "diversity for diversity's sake" feeling

## Bridge Metaphor (Implicit)

The "bridge" concept is expressed through:

1. **Composition**: Team arranged with flow from foundation (front) to growth (back)
2. **Body Language**: Hands connected/overlapping, reaching forward and upward
3. **Background Zones**: Left (foundation/stability) transitions to right (growth/ambition)
4. **Positioning**: Front members grounded, back members elevated/reaching
5. **Visual Flow**: Eye naturally follows the bridge from base to forward edge

**Not literal**: No actual bridge structure. The team members themselves form the metaphorical bridge through their arrangement and connection.

## Alternative Concept (If Needed)

If the above feels too abstract, alternative: Team standing on a modern architectural bridge at golden hour, silhouetted or backlit, with the bridge literally connecting two shores (representing foundation and growth). More literal but equally powerful.
